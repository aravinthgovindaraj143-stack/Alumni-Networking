🏛 Our Story

A Shared Legacy,
Continuously Renewed

The alumni community of Sacred Heart College, Tirupattur is built on trust, academic spirit, and collective responsibility.

What started as reconnecting old classmates has evolved into a platform for mentorship, leadership, collaboration, and institution-building.

Our community focuses on real outcomes: guiding students, supporting careers, enabling entrepreneurship, and preserving the values that shaped us.
